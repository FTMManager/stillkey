# 09 · References

Sources behind the technical claims in this plan. They were read on 2026-11-09, except for a few links added last (two UEFI sections, one EDK II file, the `sha2` crate page and three PowerShell cmdlet pages), which were taken from checked notes and not re-opened; open a link before relying on it for a decision. Where a claim could not be found in a primary source it is listed as an assumption in [01 §2](01-overview-and-architecture.md#2-assumptions-to-verify), or labelled as an assumption at the place it is used, instead of being cited here.

Citation style used in the other documents: `[UEFI §n]` means the UEFI Specification 2.11; "SDM" means the Intel manual, cited by section title because Intel renumbered Volume 3A in 2026; `KBnnnnnnn` means the Microsoft Support article with that number. Links to QEMU and EDK II files point at the releases this plan uses, QEMU v10.2.1 and edk2-stable202511.
